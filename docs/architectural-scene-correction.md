# Architectural First findings correction — September 28, 2026

## Purpose and boundaries

This implements the user's “fix it” instruction after rejecting the flat, crowded scene. The governing challenge remains Sacred Spaces, Safe Homes, original Challenge Summary PDF pages 18–20: help a non-expert leader understand sourced affordable-housing possibilities and prepare the next conversation. This correction improves the requested visual study and comprehension of its findings. It does not complete affordable delivery, professional feasibility or the outstanding editable presentation deliverables.

## What changed

- The existing Three.js renderer now constructs off-white neighborhood volumes, fits the source property and selection, chooses a diagonal architectural view with fewer obstructions, and uses consistent studio illumination. Source parcel geometry and the separate blue selection are retained. Focus, orbit, plan and neighborhood controls remain available.
- Parent building footprints no longer disappear when only some parts are mapped. Part relationships and minimum heights are retained, and covered volumes are reconciled without changing placement footprints. Roof tags are retained as source data, but detailed roof reconstruction is not implemented.
- Heights follow source evidence: existing mapped metre heights, then exact-ID supplemental Overture heights, then recorded storeys at an explicitly assumed 3 m/storey. Where neither is present, a labelled one-storey context placeholder is used. This supersedes the earlier presentation policy of flattening every building with missing metre heights. Placeholder heights are never inserted into zoning evidence or collision geometry. Clicking a building reveals its height basis.
- Supplemental heights use the pinned Overture 2026-09-23.1 inspection archive. Maintained `pmtiles`, `@mapbox/vector-tile` and `pbf` packages decode bounded byte ranges. Original OSM footprints remain authoritative for this scene. Ambiguous IDs, conflicting records, lifted parts, unsupported heights and mismatches do not receive a guessed join. Source-specific height provenance is retained, including lidar versus machine-learning estimates.
- The provider has a fixed HTTPS origin, strict range and archive checks, a 12-second deadline, size/decompression/tile/request bounds and a bounded cache. Failure retains the OSM context and a precise supplemental-height failure state. The archive is published for inspection, not as a production cartography service; this prototype integration is not a claim of nationwide production coverage or survey accuracy.
- Left-side priorities are compact and deduplicated without losing factual receipts. One scene label is expanded at a time, with separated touch targets and a closable detail panel. The right side retains the sourced headline and consequential context, with full findings and sources in dialogs. An eligible housing count comes from the calculated concept, never a decorative score.
- Gloo can choose detached homes, attached rows or a small apartment mass. Attached rows must fit as a whole. Apartments require explicit floor-plate, unit-area, units-per-floor and shared-space assumptions. Counts are area allocations, not solved room layouts, legal capacity, egress compliance or affordable delivery. Gloo still chooses tools, observes failures, revises and reviews the selected calculation; no fixed keyword or layout-selection fallback was introduced.
- Session-only saved findings restore without automatically calling Gloo. Exact input and assessment/evidence/geometry version checks reject stale scenarios. Interrupted work stays incomplete. An explicit findings/retry/refinement action still starts the requested work, including the automatic study. Old tabs loaded before this change cannot retroactively persist their private in-memory findings.
