## Changes

- Gloo selects a nearby-place category, travel mode and exact original priority excerpt. Schools, police, hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, supermarkets and bus stops have an actual mapped-place and routing tool. Walking is the labelled initial mode when unspecified. No keyword-only priority interpreter was substituted.
- Valhalla returns the street/path polyline, length and estimated travel time. The tool checks geometry, endpoint proximity and length consistency. A failed route has no distance and no straight-line fallback. Prior saved straight-line receipts are retired in both the panel and discussion brief.
- Nearby discovery checks up to three geographically close mapped candidates within the chosen search radius, then chooses the shortest successfully routed result. It is not a complete inventory or a guarantee of the globally nearest station/school. The receipt records attempted candidates, failures, mode, source and coverage. Endpoints are snapped to mapped travel networks; the approach through private land and destination entrances is unverified. Police travel distance does not measure safety or response time.
- Streets are mesh surfaces with connected bends and rounded joins, with blue-grey surfaces and a red route. Widths use mapped widths, then lane counts, then cartographic road-class estimates. This is not a surveyed roadway boundary. Mapped streets along the route are fetched separately from the immediate building context. Building footprints/heights remain source-based and are not invented along the route.
- The regulatory path follows the resolved municipality, active township, county and state. In confirmed no-comprehensive-zoning systems, a preliminary exploration requires operative new-development/plat/site-plan or residential provisions plus the official system evidence. Guidance alone, definitions, county guidance for a municipality, and unsupported affirmative permission remain rejected. Gloo still chooses research and reviews citations.
- State government discovery was added through the official government-domain directory. This is discovery, not a complete state-law applicability engine. Tribal/special authority and overriding legislation still require actual evidence.
- When Census address-geography lookup fails completely, a separate official TIGERweb adapter checks the whole selected polygon against current state/county/incorporated-place/county-subdivision layers. Both intersections and full containment must agree. It refuses incomplete, truncated and crossed-boundary results; statistical subdivisions do not become municipal governments.
